How I Help

Questions founders often ask at this stage of growth:

Product

  • Which of our SKUs are actually paying for the rest of the catalog?
  • Should we launch this new product line, or does the data say we don't have room for it yet?
  • Why is our best-selling product also our worst repeat-purchase product?
  • Are we ready to move from a hero-product strategy to a multi-product strategy?

Customer

  • What's our real LTV — the one we'd defend to an investor, not the one we've been quoting?
  • Which cohort of customers is actually growing our business, and which one is quietly flat?
  • Are our repeat customers repeating because they love the product, or because we're bribing them with discounts?
  • Why did retention improve last quarter — was it something we did, or something in the mix?

Marketing

  • Meta says our ROAS is 3x. Finance says we're losing money on new customers. Who's right?
  • Which channel is actually driving profitable growth, and which one is just taking credit for it?
  • Are we spending more on email because it's working, or because it's easy to measure?
  • If we cut paid spend by 30% next quarter, what would actually happen?

Merchandising

  • Which products deserve more inventory, and which ones are eating our cash?
  • Is our new-product launch cadence helping or fragmenting the catalog?
  • What's the real signal behind our slow-movers — bad product, bad merchandising, or bad timing?

Finance

  • Where is margin actually leaking, and how much would it cost us to plug it?
  • Is our subscription business subsidizing the one-time business, or the other way around?
  • How much of our growth this year came from price increases we forgot we made?
  • What does it actually cost us to acquire a customer, once you count everything?

AI

  • Where should we be using AI right now, and where would it be a waste of six months?
  • Our team keeps asking for "AI features." Which of those is a real opportunity versus a distraction?
  • Could we automate our reporting layer without adding a dashboard that no one reads?
