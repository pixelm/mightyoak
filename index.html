The wall

Somewhere between $300k and $5M, most ecommerce founders hit the same wall.

The questions stop being about what to build and start being about what to measure, what to trust, and what to fix. The stack you stood up in year one is now sprawling. Five apps quietly contradict each other. The dashboards are up, but you're not sure they're telling you the truth.

You're making decisions you used to make on instinct — and the business has grown past the point where instinct is enough.

A full-time CTO is overkill at this stage. Piecing together freelancers means managing five people across five platforms with no one connecting the dots. So most founders just live with the fog.

That's the wall. That's where I come in.

About

Make the technology work for the business.

I'm a technologist and entrepreneur who has spent years inside the Shopify ecosystem — talking to thousands of successful business owners, watching how they actually operate, and learning what separates the brands that compound from the ones that stall.

As Head of Data Products at Recharge, I worked directly with businesses like Lovevery, Hello Bello, Huel, and Four Sigmatic — among many others. That work taught me which technologies are effective and which ones aren't, what data actually matters from the fragmented systems most ecommerce businesses run on, and how to turn scattered signals into a coherent execution strategy.

Every business is different. But the thing that consistently makes a business strong is this: they don't orient the business model around the technology. They make the technology work for them. And they're not afraid to change their stack when their strategy outgrows it.

That kind of flexibility — being willing to let go of what you built so you can reach what's next — is what makes expansive growth possible. It's also the thing most founders need a partner for. That's the work I'm here to do.
